## 🔬 How We Calculate Confluences

### **Step 1: Fibonacci Analysis**
We calculate 43 key Fibonacci retracement and extension levels from pivot highs and lows:

**Retracements (0-100%):**
- **Critical levels:** 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% (Golden Ratio)
- **Extended ratios:** 78.6%, 88.6%, 92.2%
- **Minor levels:** 11.1%, 21.78%, 30.4%, 33.3%, etc.

**Extensions (100-200%):**
- **Projection levels:** 111%, 118%, 122.43%, 127.2%
- **Advanced targets:** 138.2%, 161.8%, 178.6%, 200%

### **Step 2: Square of Nine Calculation**
Using W.D. Gann's sacred geometry, we calculate price levels at key angular degrees:
- **Cardinal directions:** 90°, 180°, 270°, 360°
- **Diagonal angles:** 45°, 135°, 225°, 315°
- **Extended cycles:** 450°, 540°, 630°, 720°

Each degree represents a geometric relationship in price-time space.

### **Step 3: Confluence Detection**
We measure the **distance** between each Fibonacci level and its nearest Square of Nine level. When this distance is very small, we've found a confluence!

## 💪 Understanding Strength Scores

### **Strength Score System (1-4)**

Our algorithm assigns strength scores based on how closely the Fibonacci and S9 levels align:

| Score | Strength | Distance Criteria | Meaning |
|-------|----------|-------------------|---------|
| **4** | 🌟 **Perfect** | Distance = 0 | Exact alignment - extremely rare |
| **3** | ⭐ **Strong** | Distance ≤ 5.0 points | Very tight alignment - high confidence |

## 📈 Severity Score - The Quality Metric

The **severity score** combines strength with price context to give you a quality metric:

### **Formula:**
```
Severity = Strength Score / (1 + Distance Normalized by ATR)
```

**Components:**
- **Strength Score (1-4):** Base confluence strength
- **ATR (Average True Range):** Market volatility measure
- **Distance Normalization:** Adjusts for instrument-specific price scales

### **Interpreting Severity:**

| Severity Range | Quality | Action |
|----------------|---------|--------|
| **2.90 - 3.00** | 🔴 **Exceptional** | Highest priority - strong position levels |
| **2.80 - 2.89** | 🟠 **Excellent** | Very high probability zones |
| **2.70 - 2.79** | 🟡 **Good** | Reliable support/resistance |
| **< 2.70** | 🔵 **Moderate** | Secondary levels for confirmation |

**Why Severity Matters:**
- Accounts for market volatility (high volatility = looser alignment acceptable)
- Normalizes across different instruments (FX, metals, indices)
- Prioritizes confluences relative to current market conditions

## 📐 Mathematical Foundation

### **Fibonacci Sequence**
Based on the golden ratio φ (phi) ≈ 1.618, the Fibonacci sequence appears throughout nature and markets. Key ratios:
- **0.382** = φ² - φ
- **0.618** = 1/φ (Golden Ratio)
- **1.618** = φ (Golden Ratio)
- **2.618** = φ²

### **Square of Nine**
W.D. Gann's geometric system treating price and time as coordinates on a spiral. Each 360° rotation represents a complete cycle, with cardinal and diagonal angles marking critical price levels.

**Angular Relationships:**
- **45°** = 1/8 cycle (0.125 factor)
- **90°** = 1/4 cycle (0.25 factor)
- **180°** = 1/2 cycle (0.5 factor)
- **360°** = Full cycle (1.0 factor)

### **Confluence Probability**

The probability that a Fibonacci level randomly falls within our "Strong" threshold (≤5 points) of any S9 level is statistically low. When it happens, it indicates genuine mathematical harmony between the two systems.

**Statistical Significance:**
- **Random alignment:** ~2-3% probability
- **Strong confluence (≤5pt):** <1% probability
- **Perfect confluence (=0):** <0.1% probability

This rarity makes strong confluences powerful predictive tools.

   - Place stops beyond the confluence zone
