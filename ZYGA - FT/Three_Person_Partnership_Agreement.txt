Three-Person Partnership Agreement
====================================

Date: [Insert Date]
Partnership Name: [Insert Partnership Name]
Partnership Address: [Insert Address]

This Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and among the following parties (collectively, the "Partners"):

1. [Trader Name], hereinafter referred to as "Trader"
2. [Developer Name], hereinafter referred to as "Developer"
3. [Investor Name], hereinafter referred to as "Investor"

--------------------------------------------------
1. Purpose of the Partnership
The Partners agree to form a partnership to:
- Jointly invest in and manage trading accounts (starting with a Prop Firm $50,000 account)
- Generate profits from trading activities
- Develop systems, strategies, and intellectual property (IP) to enhance trading operations
- Explore future business ventures, including the potential creation of a Public Limited Company (PLC) to protect IP and formalize operations

--------------------------------------------------
2. Initial Capital Contribution
- Each Partner shall contribute $100, totaling $300.
- The capital shall be used initially to purchase a $50,000 Prop Firm account.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Ownership and Profit Sharing
- Each Partner shall own 33.33% of the partnership.
- All profits generated by the partnership shall be distributed according to the ownership percentages after deducting any mutually agreed expenses.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Partner  | Role                    | Responsibilities |
|----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Trader   | Trading & Account Management | Executes trades using personal strategies; responsible for daily account operations. |
| Developer | System Development     | Provides trading systems, tools, and software to assist the Trader; does not govern the Trader’s trading decisions. |
| Investor | Business & Future Ventures | Manages business development, funding, and partnership-related planning for future ventures. |

Note: Any contributions outside the initial capital (e.g., software development, intellectual property, additional investment) shall be documented and agreed upon in writing.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Bank Account
- The partnership shall use one specified bank account for all deposits, withdrawals, and trading account funding.
- All Partners have personal banking accounts, but all partnership transactions must flow through the partnership bank account.
- Withdrawals or deposits outside this account require unanimous written consent.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Expenses and Approvals
- Any spending on mutual interests (e.g., IP protection, PLC formation, tools) must be:
  1. Agreed upon in writing by all Partners
  2. Audited monthly during casual partnership meetings
- No Partner may unilaterally spend partnership funds outside these rules.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Profit Distribution
- Profits shall be calculated monthly after accounting for expenses.
- Each Partner is entitled to 33.33% of net profits.
- Profits shall be distributed within 5 business days of the monthly audit meeting, unless otherwise agreed.

--------------------------------------------------
8. Auditing and Reporting
- The partnership shall maintain transparent records of all income, expenses, and trading activity.
- All accounts and financial statements shall be audited monthly by all three Partners during a scheduled meeting.
- Any discrepancies must be resolved jointly.

--------------------------------------------------
9. Intellectual Property
- Trading strategies, systems, and algorithms developed for or by the partnership are considered joint intellectual property.
- The Trader retains the right to use personal strategies but agrees to document the strategy for partnership purposes if requested.
- Any use or licensing of IP for outside ventures requires unanimous written approval.

--------------------------------------------------
10. Future Ventures
- The partnership may explore forming a PLC to protect IP and expand operations.
- Decisions regarding the PLC, business registration, or external investment require full agreement from all Partners.

--------------------------------------------------
11. Termination or Withdrawal
- Any Partner wishing to leave the partnership must:
  1. Provide 30 days written notice
  2. Allow for valuation and buyout of their share based on net partnership assets at the time of exit
- Remaining Partners may buy out the exiting Partner’s share according to ownership percentages.

--------------------------------------------------
12. Dispute Resolution
- Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by:
  1. Discussion among all Partners
  2. Mediation if informal resolution fails
  3. Arbitration or legal action only as a last resort

--------------------------------------------------
13. Governing Law
