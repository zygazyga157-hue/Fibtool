## 2. LEVERAGE AND MARGIN RISKS

### 2.1 Leverage Risk

**Leverage amplifies BOTH gains and losses:**
- High leverage can result in rapid, substantial losses
- A small adverse price movement can result in total loss
- Margin calls may force liquidation at unfavorable prices
- You may lose more than your initial margin deposit

**Example:**
- 100:1 leverage means 1% adverse move = 100% loss of margin
- 50:1 leverage means 2% adverse move = 100% loss of margin
- 10:1 leverage means 10% adverse move = 100% loss of margin

### 2.2 Margin Trading

**Margin trading increases risk:**
- You are borrowing funds to trade
- Interest charges may apply
- Brokers may close positions without notice if margin is insufficient
- Volatility can trigger unexpected margin calls
- You remain liable for any deficit in your account

### 2.3 Stop-Loss Limitations

Stop-loss orders do NOT guarantee protection:
- Slippage may result in execution at worse prices
- Gap openings may bypass stop-loss levels completely
- Market volatility may prevent execution
- Broker issues may delay or prevent execution

## 3. MARKET-SPECIFIC RISKS

### 3.1 Forex (Foreign Exchange) Risks

**Currency trading involves unique risks:**
- **High Volatility:** Rapid price movements can result in significant losses
- **24-Hour Market:** Prices can gap over weekends or holidays
- **Leverage:** Typically 50:1 to 500:1, amplifying losses
- **Political Risk:** Government actions, policy changes, elections
- **Economic Risk:** Inflation, interest rates, economic data releases
- **Country Risk:** Emerging markets may have higher volatility and risk
- **Liquidity Risk:** Some currency pairs have low liquidity
- **Counterparty Risk:** Broker insolvency or default

### 3.2 Cryptocurrency Risks

**Crypto trading has EXTREME risks:**
- **Extreme Volatility:** 10-50% price swings in a single day are common
- **24/7 Market:** No trading halts; losses can occur anytime
- **Regulatory Uncertainty:** Laws may change suddenly, affecting value
- **Security Risks:** Hacking, theft, loss of private keys
- **Exchange Risk:** Exchange failures, hacks, or insolvency
- **Limited History:** Cryptocurrencies lack long-term performance data
- **Liquidity Risk:** Some cryptocurrencies have very low liquidity
- **Technology Risk:** Blockchain issues, forks, or protocol changes
- **No Central Authority:** No government backing or protection

### 3.3 Stock Index (CFD) Risks

**Index CFDs carry specific risks:**
- **Leverage Risk:** Amplified losses from margin trading
- **Overnight Fees:** Holding costs can erode profits
- **Gap Risk:** Indices can gap on market open
- **Correlation Risk:** Multiple positions may correlate during market stress
- **Dividend Adjustments:** May affect CFD pricing
- **Counterparty Risk:** CFDs are contracts with brokers, not ownership

### 3.4 Commodities Risks

**Commodities have unique characteristics:**
- **Supply/Demand Volatility:** Weather, geopolitics, production disruptions
- **Storage Costs:** Affect futures pricing
- **Seasonality:** Price patterns based on harvest, weather cycles
- **Geopolitical Risk:** Wars, sanctions, trade disputes
- **Currency Risk:** Most commodities priced in USD
- **Contango/Backwardation:** Futures curve shapes affect returns

### 3.5 Synthetic Indices Risks

**Boom/Crash/Volatility indices have EXTREME characteristics:**
- **Artificial Volatility:** Designed to simulate extreme market conditions
- **Rapid Movements:** Can move hundreds of points in seconds
- **High Risk/High Reward:** Extremely dangerous for undisciplined traders
- **Algorithm-Driven:** Price movements are computer-generated
- **No Fundamental Analysis:** Technical analysis only
- **Limited Brokers:** Only available through specific brokers

### 8.2 Mental Health

Be aware of mental health impacts:
- Significant losses can cause depression or anxiety

- Relationship strain from time commitment or losses
- Sleep disruption from 24-hour markets
- Financial stress affects overall well-being

**Seek help if:**
- Trading is affecting your mental health
- You're chasing losses compulsively
- Trading is interfering with work or relationships
