ADVANCED FIBONACCI EXPANSION + RETRACEMENT ENGINE (Pesavento Pattern
Recognition Interpretation)

Purpose This document upgrades the prior anchor logic by incorporating
concepts from Larry Pesavento’s Fibonacci work and adapting them into
programmable rules for Fib Retracements and Fib Expansions.

  --------------------
  1. CORE PHILOSOPHY
  --------------------

The market is treated as a sequence of repeating swings.

The goal is not to predict tops or bottoms.

The goal is:

1.  Identify measurable swings
2.  Measure their ratios
3.  Compare swings
4.  Project probable completion zones
5.  Control risk

The strategy assumes price swings repeat in ratio and proportion.

Anchors are not random points. Anchors represent mathematically
significant swings.

  -------------------------
  2. PIVOTS ARE STRUCTURE
  -------------------------

A pivot represents temporary completion of movement.

Pivot High: High greater than surrounding highs.

Pivot Low: Low lower than surrounding lows.

Default:

left = 5 right = 5

Program rule:

PivotHigh(i):

high[i] >= highs(i-5:i+5)

PivotLow(i):

low[i] <= lows(i-5:i+5)

Raw pivots alone are insufficient.

Noise creates many false pivots.

Therefore filtering is required.

  ---------------------
  3. SWING VALIDATION
  ---------------------

Swing:

A → B

Minimum:

ABS(B-A) >= ATR(14) × 1.5

Purpose:

Reject tiny reactions.

Pesavento repeatedly emphasized meaningful swings and symmetry rather
than insignificant movement.

Small swings distort ratio measurements.

  -------------------------------
  4. ALTERNATING STRUCTURE RULE
  -------------------------------

Valid:

low → high high → low low → high → low high → low → high

Rejected:

high → high low → low

Reason:

Fibonacci relationships require completed impulse and reaction
sequences.

  ----------------------
  5. RETRACEMENT LOGIC
  ----------------------

A = impulse origin B = impulse termination

Bullish:

A=pivot low B=pivot high

Bearish:

A=pivot high B=pivot low

Fib orientation:

0%=A 100%=B

The Fib tool orientation is created automatically by anchor ordering.

No separate 0% calculation exists.

  -----------------------

Interpretation:

Very strong impulses tend to retrace shallow.

Weak impulses tend toward deeper retracements.

  ------------------------
  8. EXPANSION STRUCTURE
  ------------------------

Expansion requires:

A-B-C

Meaning:

A=impulse start

B=impulse end

C=retracement completion

Projected D generated mathematically.

  ---------------------
  9. EXPANSION RATIOS
  ---------------------

1.272 1.618 2.618 4.236

Formula:

D=C+((B-A)*ratio)

1.272 is critical.

It often appears immediately beyond failed highs/lows.

1.618 acts as major expansion.

  -------------------
  10. FAILURE LOGIC
  -------------------

If point X is exceeded:

Assume continuation.

Projected target:

1.27 or 1.618

Continuation expansions become more probable.

  ------------------
  11. AB=CD FILTER
  ------------------

Measure:

AB

Compare:

CD

Expected:

AB≈CD

Expanded:

CD=1.27×AB

or

CD=1.618×AB

Similarity score:

ABS(AB-CD)/AB

Smaller value:

Higher confidence.

  -------------------
  12. TIME SYMMETRY
  -------------------

Measure:

Compare:

Bars(C→D)

Expected:

Equal

Expanded:

Price symmetry alone is incomplete.

Time symmetry improves quality.

  -----------------------
  13. ANCHOR CONFIDENCE
  -----------------------

confidence=weighted sum

Highest confidence anchor selected.

  ----------------------------
  14. FINAL ENGINE HIERARCHY
  ----------------------------

Detect pivots
