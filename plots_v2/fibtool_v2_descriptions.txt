# Fibtool V2 Production Description Standard

V2 objects are not only chart drawings. Each object must explain:

- what geometry created it
- why the region matters
- what reaction is expected
- what bias it implies
- what invalidates it
- which engine generated it

The production metadata contract is implemented in:

plots_v2/descriptions.py

Every emitted object should carry these metadata fields:

- description_version
- description
- summary
- purpose
- market_context
- interpretation
- expected_reaction
- trade_bias
- confidence
- confidence_score
- risk_note
- operator_note
- generated_by
- anchor_source
- components

Confidence scale:

- 0-40: WEAK
- 40-60: MODERATE
- 60-80: STRONG
- 80-90: HIGH
- 90-100: EXTREME

Production rule:

Objects describe reaction probability, not certainty. Language must avoid guaranteed target claims.

Pesavento pattern-recognition rule:

- A ratio level is useful when it completes a recognizable price/time pattern.
- The engine should communicate pattern completion, proportion, and risk; it should not imply that a line predicts the future.
- Square-root ratios matter: 0.786 is the square root of 0.618, and 1.272 is the square root of 1.618.
- Failed reaction patterns often become continuation evidence, so every description must include an invalidation idea.
- The operator question is always: is the pattern present, are the ratios present, and is the risk acceptable?

MT5 object property rule:

- MT5 object name = internal stable identifier used for cleanup, lifecycle, and parent-child relationships.
- MT5 object description = production explanation from metadata["description"].
- MT5 tooltip = compact hover summary built from description, confidence, bias, expected reaction, risk, and engine.
- OBJ_TEXT exception = OBJPROP_TEXT remains the visible chart label; the production explanation is attached through OBJPROP_TOOLTIP.
- Chart text should be minimal and only used where it improves at-a-glance chart reading.
- Confluence labels are allowed because score + Fib + S9 is useful on the chart.
- Square of Nine standalone labels and projection D marker labels are not emitted by default; their detail belongs in descriptions/tooltips.

Universal chart text rule:

- Keep chart text to one short line where possible.
- Use compact component notation, for example: `78 | Fib 0.382 | S9 -45deg`.
- Do not put purpose, reaction, risk, operator notes, or long descriptions directly on the chart.
- Full explanation belongs in MT5 object description and tooltip.

## Universal Object Template

[DESCRIPTION]

Purpose:
The job of the object.

Market Context:
The market condition that created the object.

Anchor Source:
The pivot, ABC, session, or structure source behind the object.

Components:
The geometry systems included in the object.

Confidence:
Numerical score and verbal band.

Interpretation:
What the geometry means.

Expected Reaction:
Pause, rejection, continuation, acceleration, consolidation, or timing reaction.

Trade Bias:
Bullish continuation, bearish continuation, support reaction, resistance reaction, neutral timing, or structure dependent.

Risk:
The condition that invalidates the object.

Operator Note:
How the object should be used in the workflow.

Generated By:
The engine source.

## Fibonacci Retracement

Description:
FIBONACCI RETRACEMENT DECISION REGION

Purpose:
Identify pullback zones inside an active impulse leg.

Market Context:
Generated after a significant pivot-to-pivot move where price may retrace before continuation.

Anchor Source:
Pivot A -> Pivot B selected by the confidence-ranked anchor engine.

Anchor Quality Inputs:

- ATR swing quality
- pivot quality
- liquidity sweep score
- MSS/session alignment
- recency weighting

Level Meaning:

0.236:
Very shallow retracement. Momentum remains strong.

0.382:
Healthy pullback in an active trend.

0.500:
Equilibrium region.

0.618:
Primary institutional decision region.

0.786:
Deep pullback. Continuation probability weakens and reversal risk rises.

1.000:
Full retracement of the impulse leg.

1.272:
Early expansion beyond prior structure.

1.618:
Extended continuation region.

Expected Reaction:
Treat the level as a possible pattern-completion area. Shallow reactions favor momentum, 0.500-0.618 is the main decision band, and 0.786 is a deep square-root retracement where continuation must prove itself.

Trade Bias:
Bullish continuation when Pivot B is above Pivot A. Bearish continuation when Pivot B is below Pivot A.

Risk:
A close beyond the pivot origin invalidates the retracement setup.

Operator Note:
Do not treat the ratio alone as a signal. Confirm pattern quality and define risk before execution.

Generated By:
FibRetracementEngine V2

## Fibonacci Projection

Description:
FIBONACCI PROJECTION REACTION PATH

Purpose:
Forecast expansion and reaction zones after an A-B-C structure.

Market Context:
Generated after an impulse, pullback, and continuation structure.

Anchor Source:
Pivot A -> Pivot B -> Pivot C selected from alternating pivots.

Projection Confidence:

0.35 * anchor_score
+ 0.25 * structure_score
+ 0.20 * volatility_score
+ 0.20 * timeframe_alignment

Then adjusted by instrument weight.

Level Meaning:

127.2:
First continuation checkpoint.

161.8:
Primary institutional expansion region.

261.8:
Strong acceleration region.

423.6:
Extreme expansion region.

Expected Reaction:
1.272, the square root of 1.618, is the first expansion checkpoint. 1.618 is the golden-mean expansion. 2.618 and 4.236 mark stretched continuation where reaction or acceleration risk increases.

Trade Bias:
Continuation until Pivot C or the active structure fails.

Risk:
A close beyond Pivot C invalidates the active projection path.

Operator Note:
Use the projection to compare profit potential against the defined failure point. It is not a standalone prediction.

Generated By:
FibProjectionEngine V2

## Projected D Reaction Level

Description:
PROJECTED D REACTION LEVEL

Purpose:
Mark a projected expansion price derived from C plus the A-B impulse.

Market Context:
Generated from the same A-B-C structure as the parent projection object.

Expected Reaction:
Expect a decision rather than a guaranteed target. Price may pause, reject, or accelerate if the pattern remains valid into the D area.

Trade Bias:
Continuation checkpoint.

Risk:
Invalid when the parent projection structure fails.

Operator Note:
Use the parent ABC quality and risk/reward before acting on an individual D marker.

Generated By:
FibProjectionEngine V2

## Confluence Zone

Description:
CONFLUENCE REACTION ZONE

Purpose:
Merge independent geometry systems into a single reaction region.

Components:

- Fibonacci price level
- Square of Nine rotational level
- timeframe alignment
- optional structure context
- optional projection context

Interpretation:
Multiple independent measurements point to the same price region, creating a stronger pattern-completion candidate than any single level.

Expected Reaction:
Expect a behavioral change at the zone: hesitation, rejection, consolidation, or a continuation break. A clean failure through the zone often supports continuation.

Trade Bias:
Resistance reaction when the zone is above current price. Support reaction when the zone is below current price. Bias can be overridden by dominant structure.

Risk:
A decisive close beyond the zone invalidates the immediate reaction expectation.

Operator Note:
Highest value when ratio, pattern, and risk all line up. Do not force the setup if the structure is not obvious.

Generated By:
ConfluenceEngine V2

Example Label:

EXTREME CONFLUENCE RESISTANCE
Score: 99
Fib 1.618 + S9 180deg
Distance: 9.35
Bias: Moderate bearish reaction
Anchor: H1 Pivot Pair | Align 0.82

## Square of Nine

Description:
SQUARE OF NINE ROTATIONAL LEVEL

Purpose:
Convert price into rotational geometry around a pivot.

Important Degrees:

- 45
- 90
- 180
- 270
- 360

Interpretation:
The level acts like a vibratory price measurement around the pivot. Repeated swings near the same rotation increase its usefulness.

Expected Reaction:
Use the level as a possible profit-projection or stop-placement reference. Reaction quality improves when it aligns with Fibonacci or structure.

Trade Bias:
Support below price. Resistance above price.

Risk:
A clean close through the level weakens the immediate reaction case.

Operator Note:
Use S9 levels as geometry context. Confluence zones are preferred for action.

Generated By:
Square9Engine V2

## Gann Fan

Description:
GANN FAN PRICE/TIME EQUILIBRIUM

Purpose:
Map directional price/time balance from a selected pivot.

Market Context:
Generated from the latest qualified pivot using ATR or point-based scale.

Interpretation:
Fan angles compare price movement with time, helping judge whether the swing is maintaining proportion or losing momentum.

Expected Reaction:
The 1x1 area is a balance line. Holding it supports the active swing; losing it warns that momentum and proportion are changing.

Trade Bias:
Bullish above equilibrium. Bearish below equilibrium.

Risk:
Invalid when the selected pivot is replaced by a stronger structure.

Operator Note:
Use with retracement and confluence layers. Fan geometry alone is context.

Generated By:
GannFanEngine V2

GANN GRID PRICE/TIME MAP

Description:
FIBONACCI TIME WINDOW

Purpose:
Project future timing windows from the selected swing.

Market Context:
Generated from the same A-B swing used by price geometry.

Interpretation:
Time ratios estimate when a wave may complete or change behavior, complementing price-ratio analysis.

Watch for acceleration, consolidation, or reversal only when the timing window coincides with price structure or confluence.

Trade Bias:

Risk:

ASIA STRUCTURE RANGE

Purpose:
Show the session range that may define liquidity and breakout behavior.

Market Context:

Interpretation:
The range frames sweep, breakout, and mean-reversion behavior after Asia.

Expected Reaction:

Neutral until sweep or break direction is confirmed.

Operator Note:
Use sweeps as anchor context for retracement and confluence generation.

## Liquidity Sweep Markers

High Sweep Description:
ASIA HIGH LIQUIDITY SWEEP

High Sweep Interpretation:
Buy-side liquidity was taken. Reaction risk increases if price fails to hold above the range.

High Sweep Bias:
Bearish reaction unless price accepts above the swept high.

High Sweep Risk:
Invalid when price accepts and closes above the sweep region.

Low Sweep Description:
ASIA LOW LIQUIDITY SWEEP

Low Sweep Interpretation:
Sell-side liquidity was taken. Reaction risk increases if price fails to hold below the range.

Low Sweep Bias:
Bullish reaction unless price accepts below the swept low.

Low Sweep Risk:
Invalid when price accepts and closes below the sweep region.

Operator Note:
Check MSS or displacement before treating the sweep as reversal evidence.

Generated By:
StructureOverlayEngine V2

## Instrument Normalization

Instrument weights adjust confidence to account for noise and behavior differences:

- BTCUSD: 0.85
- US30: 0.96
- XAUUSD: 0.93
- EURUSD: 0.98
- unknown instruments: 1.00

Formula:

adjusted_score = base_score * instrument_weight

Purpose:
BTC receives a lower weight because liquidity spikes and volatility can overstate raw geometry quality. Cleaner instruments receive less penalty.

## Production Rollout Standard

Every new V2 object type must include:

1. A registry entry in plots_v2/descriptions.py.
2. Metadata generated through build_description_metadata().
3. A clear risk_note.
4. A clear operator_note.
5. A generated_by engine name.
6. Components that explain the geometry stack.

Final evolution:

Draw chart objects
-> Draw geometry
-> Explain geometry
-> Predict reaction behavior
-> Communicate trading intent
